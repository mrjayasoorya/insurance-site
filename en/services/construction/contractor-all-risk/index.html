Frequently asked questions

Questions people ask ›
What does Contractor All Risk (CAR) insurance cover? CAR covers physical loss or damage to contract works during construction — including materials, labour-related damage, and third-party liability at the worksite. It does not cover defective design or wear and tear. Coverage is for the construction period plus a maintenance period.
Who needs CAR insurance — contractor or project owner? Either can buy it, but typically the contractor is required to arrange CAR insurance and name the project owner as co-insured. Many government and large private contracts mandate CAR cover as a tender condition.
What is the sum insured for a CAR policy? Sum insured is the full contract value including materials, labour, and contractor's profit. Underinsuring creates the same average clause risk as fire insurance — partial claims get proportionately reduced.
A crane accident happened at our site — who is liable, and does CAR cover it? CAR's third-party liability section can cover injury or property damage caused at the worksite during the contract period, but crane operation is often better covered under a specific liability or crane/CPM add-on depending on how the incident happened. Report immediately, preserve the site as-is where safe, and get us the incident details — we'll check which section of your cover actually applies before you make any claim commitments.
